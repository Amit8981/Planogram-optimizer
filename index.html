🚀 Quick Start: 4-Step Merchandising Workflow

STEP 1

Select Assortment (Zone 1)

Filter products by Category, Brand, Pack Size, or Sugar. Set each SKU's Inclusion Priority (Must Have / Nice to Have / Must Not Have) and customize Min Facings.

STEP 2

Sequence Brand Flow (Zone 2)

Drag brand cards or use quick presets (⚡ By Velocity, 💰 By Margin, 🔤 A-Z) to define the horizontal left-to-right brand sequence.

STEP 3

Choose Fixture & Shelves

Select cooler model (1-Door, 2-Door, 3-Door) and adjust the number of shelves or vertical clearance heights (mm) to fit your store fixtures.

STEP 4

Choose Goal & Run Solver

Pick an optimization goal (Profit, Revenue, or Volume) and hit 🚀 RUN SOLVER to generate an optimal planogram with 0% width overflow!

📊 KPIs, Space Utilization & Export Formats

📦 Space Utilization vs Effective Capacity

Shelf Width Full (97.4%): Demonstrates that front-facing shelf width is packed to maximum commercial capacity.

Effective Capacity (89.2%): Measures 3D volume fill against maximum storable space (excluding retail cold air draft gap).

📏 Vertical Height & Headroom Utilization

Height Fill %: Calculates the vertical clearance utilized by placed product cans and bottles ($\frac{\text{Product Height}}{\text{Shelf Clearance}} \times 100\%$).

Air Gap Headroom (mm): Tracks residual space above the cans/cartons ensuring shopper hand-grab ergonomics and proper refrigeration circulation.
